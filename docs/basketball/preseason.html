2026–27 preseason rankings

Where the model starts every team, before a game of the season has been played.

Each team’s Rating is the prior its season starts from: last season’s rating, adjusted for Returning (the share of last season’s win shares still on the roster), Incoming (what the newcomers produced last season at other schools, in win shares) and the Freshman class (rated recruits above a baseline). The games then move each rating away from it, quickly: by a fifth of the season the games have taught the model most of what the prior was guessing. Out of sample, with a roster it misses a team’s final rating by about 4.2 points, against 5.1 for the old rule of last season’s rating pulled toward average.

Rosters are posted for 364 of 365 teams, so these ratings use them. Where a school has not cleaned up its list the page can still read some departed players as returning. The prior is deliberately cautious in scale, so these ratings are closer together than they will be by March. The method page has the detail.
